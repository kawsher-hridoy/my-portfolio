02 — Systems

Taken to the stage.

Four builds behind the 2026 results — two with public source.

01

Darktrace3

Tells stealer-malware victims exactly what to lock down.

Most breach checkers only tell you an email address turned up in a leak. Darktrace3 works from malware and infostealer logs instead, so it can tell a person they were compromised by a stealer at all — and show which sites and accounts were exposed, so they know precisely what to change. Passwords are always masked; the tool is built for awareness, not harvesting.

Flask · SQLite

CSAD 2026 · Champion Source private
02

Niro

Patient-owned medical records, read aloud in Bangla.

Patients own their records; an AI reads lab reports and prescriptions in plain Bangla, tracks values over time, and prepares a case summary so a real doctor can review it cheaply. The AI never gives final medical advice — it explains and flags, and consent is a core feature rather than a checkbox.

FastAPI · Next.js · Azure OpenAI

IEEE ICADHI 2026 · 1st Runners-up Source private
03

Autopilot

A GPU cluster that heals itself before it fails.

Reads GPU telemetry, predicts a failure about 100 seconds ahead, then cordons the node and migrates the running job automatically — a closed loop from prediction to recovery. Measured against the alert rule real clusters use today, on identical held-out data, with both numbers published.

DCGM · LightGBM · Kubernetes

AI Innovation Hackathon 2026 · Finalist Source
04

AI Mentor

Finds the student who is about to fall behind.

A deterministic engine scores every mentee across eight academic and attendance signals, then an AI turns the numbers into a plain-language brief. Mentors open a ranked command centre instead of scrolling a flat table — and every score can be audited back to the rule that produced it.

04 — Approach

Three rules that show up in the code.

01

Deterministic where it counts

In AI Mentor the risk score comes from eight auditable signals — the model only narrates it. Every score traces back to the rule that produced it, so a mentor can ask why and get an answer instead of a probability.

02

AI explains, people decide

Niro reads lab reports and prescriptions in Bangla and prepares a case summary, but it never gives final medical advice. It flags and explains; a doctor reviews. Consent and audit trails are features, not footnotes.

03

Measure against the baseline that exists

Autopilot was scored against the alert rule real clusters use today, on identical held-out data, and both numbers were published. A model is only impressive next to the thing it replaces.
